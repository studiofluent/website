About 🧍‍♂️

Hi, I'm Ryan, the lead tutor at Studio Fluent. I hold a BA in Modern Languages (German and French) and have experience teaching both adults and children, from supporting students at university to my own tutoring practice.

I've previously worked in HR systems, the arts, and the charity sector, communicating with people from a wide range of cultural backgrounds. Outside of work, my hobbies include running, bouldering and reading.

What I enjoy most about tutoring languages is seeing the excitement from students learning about a new culture. Whatever your reasons for learning, my aim is to provide friendly, practical lessons that increase your confidence using your new language.

It is important that you like both your course and your tutor, so I am more than happy to arrange a call and discuss how I teach and what you would like from the course.
